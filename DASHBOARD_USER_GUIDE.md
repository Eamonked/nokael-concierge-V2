## 📋 Understanding the Jobs Tab

### The Top Stats (3 colored boxes)
- **Active** (Red/Orange) - Jobs in progress right now - **NEEDS ATTENTION**
- **Pending** (Yellow) - Jobs waiting to be assigned
- **Completed** (Green) - Finished jobs today

### The 4 Columns
Think of these as your **delivery pipeline**:

```
PENDING → IN TRANSIT → COMPLETED → CANCELLED
  ⏳          🚚            ✅           ❌
```

1. **Pending** - New jobs that need a driver assigned
2. **In Transit** - Jobs currently being delivered
3. **Completed** - Successful deliveries
4. **Cancelled** - Jobs that didn't happen (with reason)

### Job Cards Explained
Each card shows:
- **Job ID** (example: NOK-0064) - Use this to track it
- **Route** - Where it's going (Dubai → Abu Dhabi)
- **Driver name** - Who's delivering it
- **Status badges** - Color-coded progress indicators

## 💬 Understanding the Quotes Tab

### What's a Quote?
A **quote** is when someone fills out the "Request a Quote" form on your website. They want to know if you can deliver something.

### The 3 Stats at Top
- **New** (Red/Orange) - Fresh requests - **RESPOND TODAY**
- **Total** - All quotes ever received
- **Completed** - Quotes you turned into actual jobs

### What You See
A table with columns:
- **Customer name & phone**
- **What they want delivered** (Item Type)
- **Where** (Pickup → Delivery)
- **When** (How urgent)
- **Status** (Pending/Contacted/Completed/Lost)

### What You Should Do

#### For NEW quotes (Pending):
1. **Call the customer** using the phone number shown
2. **Click "Mark Contacted"** after you call
3. If they want to proceed → **Click "Convert to Job"**
4. If they don't want to proceed → **Click "Mark as Lost"** and select why

#### For CONTACTED quotes:
- Either convert to job or mark as lost
- Don't leave them in "Contacted" status for more than 24 hours

#### For LOST quotes:
- These are done. They show why we lost them (competitor, price, etc.)
- Use this data to improve your pricing/service

## 🚗 Understanding the Drivers Tab

### The 3 Stats at Top
- **Needs Review** (Red/Orange) - New applications - **REVIEW THIS WEEK**
- **Total** - Everyone who ever applied
- **Active** - Approved drivers ready to work

### Driver Statuses Explained

| Status | What It Means | What You Do |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Pending** 🟡 | Just applied, not reviewed yet | Review documents, approve or reject |
| **Approved** 🟢 | Verified and can take jobs | Nothing - they're ready |
| **Rejected** 🔴 | Not qualified | Nothing - they're out |

### How to Review a Driver Application

1. **Click on their name** to see full details
2. **Check their documents**:
   - Emirates ID (must be valid)
   - License (must match vehicle type)
   - Vehicle registration
3. **Look at these red flags**:
   - ⚠️ Documents expired
   - ⚠️ Poor quality photos (can't read text)
   - ⚠️ Wrong vehicle type for their license
4. **Make a decision**:
   - ✅ **Approve** if all documents look good
   - ❌ **Reject** if something's wrong (add reason)
   - ⏸️ **Ask for more** if documents are unclear

### Driver Availability
You'll see these status indicators on approved drivers:
- 🟢 **Available** - Free to take jobs RIGHT NOW
- 🟡 **On Job** - Currently delivering something
- ⚪ **Offline** - Not working today

## 🏢 Understanding the Business Tab

### What's a Business Client?
These are **companies** (not individuals) who want regular deliveries. They get:
- A corporate code for tracking
- Priority support
- Bulk invoicing

### The 3 Stats at Top
- **New** (Red/Orange) - Fresh inquiries - **CALL WITHIN 24H**
- **Total** - All business inquiries
- **Active** - Companies you're working with now

### What You Should Do

#### For NEW business inquiries:
1. **Call the company contact person** (phone shown)
2. **Understand their needs** (volume, frequency, budget)
3. **Update status**:
   - **Active** if you agree to work together
   - **Rejected** if not a good fit

#### For ACTIVE business clients:
- These are your VIPs
- Respond to them first
- Keep their corporate code handy (they'll use it when booking)

## 👥 Understanding the Team Tab

### What This Tab Does
Control who can access this dashboard (security).

### Team Member Roles

| Role | What They Can Do |
|------|------------------|
| **Owner** 👑 | Everything - can't be changed or removed |
| **Admin** ⚙️ | Everything except removing owner |
| **Operator** 📋 | View and update jobs, quotes, drivers |
| **Viewer** 👀 | Only view, can't change anything |

### How to Add Someone
1. Click **"Invite Team Member"**
2. Enter their **email address**
3. Choose their **role** (start with Viewer if unsure)
4. Click **"Send Invite"**
5. They'll get an email to create their account

### How to Remove Someone
1. Find their name in the list
2. Click the **trash icon** 🗑️
3. Confirm the removal

**⚠️ Warning**: Be careful with Admin role - they can do almost everything!

## 🚨 Common Problems & Solutions

### "I can't see any jobs"
✅ **Solution**: 
1. Check if you're on the right tab (click "Jobs" on left sidebar)
2. Check the filter buttons at top - click "All" to see everything
3. Clear the search box if you typed something

### "A job disappeared"
✅ **Solution**: 
- It's probably in another column (Completed or Cancelled)
- Check the filter buttons - click "All"
- Use search box to find it by job ID

### "I accidentally clicked something"
✅ **Solution**: 
- Most actions can be undone
- For cancelled jobs, click "Reactivate"
- For lost quotes, click "Reopen"
- Ask your admin if you need help

### "The numbers don't match"
✅ **Solution**: 
- Click the refresh button (🔄) at top
- The dashboard updates automatically but sometimes needs a nudge

### "I deleted something by mistake"
❌ **Problem**: 
- Deletions are permanent - can't be undone
- This is why the delete button asks "Are you sure?"
- **Always read the confirmation popup before clicking "Delete"**

## ❓ FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Q: Can I access this from home?**  
A: Yes! Works anywhere with internet.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**  
A: Most things can be undone. Ask your manager.

**Q: How do I print a job receipt?**  
A: Click the job, then click the download/print icon.

**Q: Can I see who made changes?**  
A: Yes, check the "Notes" or "History" section (if available).

**Q: Why do some customers have a crown icon?**  
A: They're business clients (VIPs) - prioritize them!

**Q: What's the difference between "Mark as Lost" and "Cancel"?**  
A: "Lost" = quote never became a job. "Cancel" = job was created but didn't complete.

**Q: Can I assign multiple drivers to one job?**  
A: No - one driver per job. If you need relay delivery, create connected jobs.
