## Final Typography Scale
The dashboard now uses a highly readable scale:

```

- Hero (H1): 38-50px (responsive)

- Medium (H3): 18-19px
- Small (H4): 17px

Body:
- Large: 16px (primary content)
- Regular: 15px (UI text, buttons)
- Small: 14px (labels, metadata)
- Micro: 13px (tiny details, legends)

Code/Mono:
- Regular: 15px
- Small: 14px
```
